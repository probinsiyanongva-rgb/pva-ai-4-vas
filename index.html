This course is not primarily an AI-tools course. It is practical VA training that happens to use AI as a productivity layer — the central skill is judgment, not prompting.

One workflow, used everywhere in this course

Every module comes back to the same seven-stage workflow. Learning to move through it — and knowing when to stop and check — is most of what this course is teaching.

Understand

What is the client actually asking for?

Prepare

What does AI actually need to help?

Define the Result

What should a useful output look like?

Use AI

Get a draft — not a finished deliverable.

Audit

Check it against the source. Trust nothing by default.

Correct

Fix what's wrong. This is still your job.

Deliver

You own what you send the client.

AI can help you do the work. You are still responsible for the work.

The course

Seven modules, in order, culminating in an integrated Module 7 capstone with your fictional client, BrightPath Learning Co., followed by a portfolio and AI-Use Explanation lesson.

  1. Not started

    AI-Assisted VA Mindset & Responsible Use

    Where AI helps, where it needs caution, protecting client information, and why confident AI output can still be wrong.
  2. Not started

    Getting Useful Results From AI

    Starting with the actual task, giving AI the right context, defining a useful result, and working in rounds.
  3. Not started

    AI for Communication

    Client emails, customer service, tone, international clients, async updates, scheduling, and removing AI slop.
  4. Not started

    AI for Research, Summarizing & Information

    Using AI as a research assistant, summarizing, extracting information, verifying before you use it, and building a research brief.
  5. Not started

    AI for Admin, Documentation & Operations

    SOPs, checklists, reports, spreadsheet assistance, and process improvement.
  6. Not started

    Choosing the Right AI Tool

    ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude — comparing strengths, staying tool-neutral, and choosing a tool for the job.
  7. Not started

    Proving Your AI-Assisted VA Skills

    The BrightPath capstone — build the AI-assisted client operations package, audit and finalize it, and document your AI use honestly.

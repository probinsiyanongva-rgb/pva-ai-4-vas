By the end of this course, you'll be able to

  • Recognize where AI can genuinely help with VA work — and where it should be used with caution, or not at all
  • Give AI useful context while protecting client information
  • Define what a useful result looks like and work with AI in multiple rounds
  • Audit AI-generated work and catch unsupported claims before you rely on them
  • Write professional client communication, adapted for async and international work
  • Use AI for research without treating it as the source of truth
  • Assist with spreadsheets, SOPs, and reports while verifying the result yourself
  • Compare AI tools and choose the right one for the task, without becoming loyal to one
  • Complete a realistic capstone and document your AI use honestly

This course is not an AI-tools course. It's practical VA training that happens to use AI as a productivity layer — the central skill is judgment, not prompting.

One principle, used everywhere in this course

Every module comes back to the same seven-stage workflow. Learning to move through it — and know when to stop and check — is most of what this course is teaching.

Understand
What is the client actually asking for?
Prepare
What does AI actually need to help?
Define the Result
What should a useful output look like?
Use AI
Get a draft — not a finished deliverable.
Audit
Check it against the source. Trust nothing by default.
Correct
Fix what's wrong. This is still your job.
Deliver
You own what you send the client.

AI can help you do the work. You are still responsible for the work.
